  in cc-hdrm/cc-hdrm/App/AppDelegate.swift, the SwiftUI flyout in cc-hdrm/cc-hdrm/Views/PopoverView.swift, the analytics window in cc-hdrm/cc-hdrm/    
  Views/AnalyticsWindow.swift, and the macOS integrations for Keychain, notifications, login items, and browser launch.

  That gives you the best shot at keeping the same clean, reactive tray-first UX without dragging in a web wrapper.
